Generated by `e2e:tables --source latency` from the latency runs that `docs/evaluation/runs.json` lists as DS2. Evidence label LIVE (whisky-v2-eval3). The primary clock (block timestamp of `ONFTSent` to block timestamp of `ONFTReceived`) and the continuity clock (block timestamp of the send to the first 5 s poll) are pooled each within itself and never with each other. Intervals appear only from n = 10. Outliers are Tukey-fence counts and stay in the sample. Chain clocks can differ by seconds and their skew is not measured.

The four hops of a window are back to back on one token, so they are not independent draws; a pooled interval is optimistic and describes these windows, not the pathway in general.
